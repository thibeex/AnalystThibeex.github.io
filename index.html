About Me

I am a results-driven Data Analyst with hands-on experience extracting actionable insights from complex datasets across multiple industries. My technical stack includes R for statistical computing and machine learning, SQL Server for data extraction, transformation, and querying at scale, Power BI for building dynamic, interactive dashboards, and Excel for advanced data modelling and reporting.

I specialise in the full data pipeline — from raw data ingestion and cleaning, through exploratory analysis and feature engineering, to the deployment of machine learning models such as K-Means clustering for customer segmentation and NLP models for sentiment analysis. My work is grounded in statistical rigour, with a focus on producing outputs that are not only analytically sound but directly applicable to business decision-making.

Holding a B.Tech in Surveying and Geoinformatics from the Federal University of Technology, Minna and an ALX Africa Data Analytics certification, I combine academic foundation with practical expertise to deliver precise, data-driven solutions. I am currently pursuing a Masters in Data Science to deepen my expertise in advanced statistical modelling and applied machine learning at scale.
